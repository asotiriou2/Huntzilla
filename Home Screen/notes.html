Our Story!

This page is dedicated to show the progress our team has made, as well as each individual member contributions.

When our team formed for CO1034, we had a blank canvas and a simple goal: build an interactive treasure hunt quiz. We quickly realized the real challenge wasn't just writing code, but learning to work as one unit. Our first hurdle was mastering Git and GitHub—learning to pull, commit, and push without stepping on each other's toes. Once we nailed the workflow, we divided the work: building the HTML foundation, styling the landing page, and fixing countless CSS Flexbox headaches. Huntzilla turned out to be more than just a web app. It was a crash course in teamwork, problem-solving, and learning how to turn a blank screen into something functional. We faced bugs, we fixed them, and in the end, we built something we're proud of.
